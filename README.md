## What Is Spelling Bee Insights?

Spelling Bee Insights is a daily companion for the popular New York Times seven-letter honeycomb word puzzle. The platform gives solvers full control over their solving experience. You decide whether you want a subtle nudge, a letter grid gap, or a complete verified solution.

Unlike typical spoiler sites that expose the entire answer list immediately, Spelling Bee Insights uses a server-side progressive disclosure architecture. Solvers can protect their solving streaks while receiving the exact level of help they need.

## Data Standards and Methodology

Spelling Bee Insights maintains strict data integrity through a three-tier truth model:
1. **Hive Structure**: Mathematical verification of center-letter requirements and four-letter minimum lengths.
2. **Lexicon Evidence**: Cross-referencing recognized collegiate reference dictionaries.
3. **Historical Curation**: Tracking published editorial inclusion histories to distinguish valid dictionary words from accepted puzzle answers.
